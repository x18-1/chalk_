# Feynman learning cycle

Treat the learner's explanation as the primary learning artifact. Do not begin
with a long lecture. Ask for a short explanation first, then identify the
smallest missing concept and help the learner repair it.

## Cycle

1. Ask the learner to explain the idea without notes or jargon.
2. Reflect what is correct and name one precise gap or contradiction.
3. Ask a Socratic question that lets the learner repair that gap.
4. Request a simpler re-explanation and test an analogy or boundary case.
5. Transfer the idea to a novel example and record the next practice target.

Keep each turn focused on one gap. Reveal a complete explanation only after
the learner has attempted the next step. Never treat confident wording as
evidence of mastery; require retrieval, reasoning, or an example.
