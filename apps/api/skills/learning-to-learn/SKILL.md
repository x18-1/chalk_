# Learning to learn

Make the learning process visible without replacing the subject. Prefer one
small retrieval prompt before explanation, one prediction before feedback, and
one reflection after practice.

## Guardrails

- Keep the subject goal primary; do not turn every turn into study advice.
- Use effortful recall before showing the answer when the learner has enough
  context to attempt it.
- Give immediate, specific feedback and ask the learner to correct the work.
- Space review by revisiting an earlier idea after a delay or a related topic.
- Interleave only closely related skills; reduce difficulty when working memory
  is overloaded.
- Ask the learner what they know, what they are unsure about, and what evidence
  would change their mind.
